## Task 2C: all-in conditional expectation and chance controls

All-in adjustment exactly averages the existing engine evaluator and settlePots payouts over every remaining board. No independent payout rules. It applies only once no further betting is possible before the river, including a covering player versus all-in opponents; other hands retain raw returns. Future boards are uniform conditional on the current board and all dealt holes, including folded cards. A folded hero's payout is deterministically zero and needs no enumeration.

The 990-board limit is postflop only. Heads-up preflop uses C(48,5)=1,712,304 boards; at six/eight seats folded dead cards reduce that count. Exact AsAh/KcKd equity is .8125548967940273, full enumeration 56.05 seconds in the initial golden run. Turn two-out, three-way side-pot and odd-chip tests also pass. The fixed 20-seed, 960-hand diagnostic has adjusted-minus-raw paired mean −.55258 bb/hand, 95% seed-t interval [−1.73533,.63018]; each arm separately includes zero. Hand variance ratios are .87004 (50) and .90075 (128). This is a diagnostic; exact conditional expectation, not a nonsignificant test, establishes the unbiasedness argument.

AIVAT-style chance-node design is in evaluation-2026-11-09-r2/AIVAT-DESIGN.md. Defer broader implementation: a frozen cheap value function is missing, and exhaustive future betting continuations may cost more than they save. No AIVAT improvement is assumed in power calculations.

## Decision summary and gates to re-present

The original roughly 292-hour figure used a narrower six-seat pilot, nominal alpha .05 and an optimistic independence approximation. The new estimates use a different frozen panel, explicit seed clusters, Bonferroni 3 and an upper-variance sensitivity. They are not a like-for-like hardware-only speedup claim. At true +5, the executable full-rotation two-arm raw design still needs **139.56 hours**; a balanced single-rotation paired counterfactual needs about **85 hours**. Neither fits 48 hours.

Within the existing three-arm scope, a true +20 primary effect gives 13.09 test hours, 26.53 predicted whole-plan hours, or 33.36 hours if every planned run consumes its full two-hour slot. A +10 effect does not fit. Under the current variance/cost proxy, the reserved-slot budget needs an assumed primary effect of roughly +14.8 bb/100 or more; this is a budget threshold, not evidence that a model achieves it. Task 4.0 must calibrate a defensible effect before N is frozen.

The stronger at-least-+5 design at true +20 totals 47.36 reserved hours, leaving only 0.64 hours. It is fragile to unmeasured model cost/variance and is not the default commitment. Doubling both variance and per-hand cost multiplies test work by about four; the superiority-at-+20 whole-plan prediction would then exceed 48 hours.

Concrete alternative if Task 4.0 supports only about +10: freeze one independently selected heuristic candidate and confirm only it versus v1. The proxy requires 4,160,520 physical hands, 18 runs, 26.16 test hours. Skipping labels/model/ONNX and reserving 2 hours for the heuristic screen plus 0.5 hours for legal/latency checks gives approximately 33.10 predicted / 44.86 reserved whole-plan hours. This changes the final family from three contrasts to one and requires owner approval; it was not implemented or run here. A balanced single-rotation confirmation mode is another potential cost reduction, but needs explicit implementation and smoke verification before use.

Gates requiring review: (1) the newly specified **5 bb/100 noninferiority margin**, one adoption contrast with a 95% two-sided lower bound above −5; (2) scaling used fixed 570-second predictions and a 600-second cap, with completed windows 512–567 seconds and winner 540 seconds, rather than pretending exact ten-minute completion; the selected-worker pilot subsequently ran 25.45 minutes continuously; (3) any proposed reduction of the three-comparison family to one. Original cost ratio <=3, inference/decision latency, sampling candidate choice and offline model gates otherwise retain their accepted numbers.

Power figures remain planning approximations. The frozen pool is exactly the two declared heuristic/baseline-v0 strata; claims do not generalize to omitted historical fields or humans. New pool membership, model policies or hardware/power conditions require new variance/cost checks. The observed AC/battery and thermal states are archived, including the sleep-interrupted diagnostic and its corrected replay.
